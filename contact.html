Clear Answers for Decision Makers

What is your typical project delivery timeline?

Rapid single-pillar deliverables (such as a Jamstack landing page or brand identity guidelines) typically take 10 to 14 business days. Comprehensive full-stack platforms and multi-service omnichannel rollouts range from 3 to 6 weeks, with clear weekly milestone checkpoints.

Do you direct both visual design and full-stack software development?

Yes. That is the core advantage of partnering with Md. Shakibur Rahaman. You eliminate cross-vendor finger-pointing because the creative vision in Figma translates 100% into clean, high-performance production code without compromise.

How are payments and project milestones structured?

Projects generally operate on a 50% initial commitment deposit to secure the development calendar, followed by 50% upon final production deployment and IP handover. For enterprise advisory and multi-month campaigns, monthly retainers are available.

Do you sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)?

Absolutely. Corporate confidentiality and intellectual property sovereignty are guaranteed. Mutual NDAs are readily executed before proprietary blueprints or strategy discussions begin.

Who will I communicate with during the project?

You communicate directly with Md. Shakibur Rahaman. There are no inexperienced account managers or junior buffers. You have direct founder-level access via WhatsApp, Slack, or Google Meet.
