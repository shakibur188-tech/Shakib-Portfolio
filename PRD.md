# Product Requirements Document (PRD) — shakibur.info

## 1. Project Overview & Mission
**shakibur.info** is the official digital flagship and portfolio platform for **Md. Shakibur Rahaman** — a Senior Digital Marketing Specialist, Technical Growth Architect, and Full-Stack Web Developer (MERN Stack, Laravel, WordPress).

The platform demonstrates the convergence of **high-performance software engineering** and **data-driven marketing acquisition systems**, providing corporate enterprises, high-growth startups, and agencies with proof of technical execution, live client platforms, and end-to-end delivery frameworks.

## 3. Core Features & Functional Scope

### 3.1 Public Portfolio Experience
- **Interactive Ambient Light & Visual Hero**: Fluid typography, animated typewriter specializations, dynamic status badges, and social verification channels.
- **9 Core Service Pillars**:
  1. *Branding & Corporate Strategy*
  2. *Graphics Design & Visual Identity (Packaging, UI/UX, Decks)*
  3. *Web Design & Full-Stack Development (MERN, Laravel, Jamstack)*
  4. *Data-Driven Social Media Marketing (SMM & Paid Meta Funnels)*
  5. *Commercial Photoshoot & Cinematic Videography*
  6. *Event Activation & 3D Spatial Stalls*
  7. *Google Ads & Intent Scaling (Search, Display, Performance Max)*
  8. *SEO & Generative AI Search Optimization (AEO, Schema Graphs)*
  9. *Strategic Public Relations (PR) & Mainstream Media Outreach*
- **15 Live Web Development Portals Directory**: Category filtering (Hospitality, E-Commerce, Corporate B2B, SaaS, Creative), live URL testing links, tech stack badges, and impact metrics.
- **Interactive Before / After Media Sliders**: Visual transformation demonstrations for branding, redesigns, and asset engineering.
- **4-Phase Delivery Framework**: Interactive stage-by-stage stepper (Discovery & Auditing, Architecture & Strategy, Engineering & Implementation, Omnichannel Scale) with progress indicator.
- **Client Endorsements & Testimonials**: 8-column visual photo mosaic with verified executive client quotes, star ratings, and roles.
- **Dynamic Contact & Scope Brief Builder**: Real-time validated project brief submission with automated backend lead capture and WhatsApp direct routing.

### 3.2 Dynamic Headless CMS & Admin Control Center (`/admin`)
- **JWT & Session Protected Authentication**: Secure credential validation for site administration.
- **Real-Time Content Management**: Edit services, web projects, creative media, milestones, and metadata without code redeployments.
- **Visual Form Builder Engine & 20+ Pre-built Templates**: Drag-and-drop form builder, public slug generation, iFrame embed codes, and submission ledger.
- **AI Studio & LLM Copywriting Suite**: Integrated OpenAI-compatible gateway (xkiro, OpenAI, OpenRouter) for automatic case study drafting, SEO tag generation, and DALL-E 3 visual assets.
- **Comprehensive SEO & AEO Command Center**: Real-time Google SERP simulator, dynamic `/llms.txt` generation, schema JSON-LD formatter, robots directives, and GA4/GTM injection.
- **Self-Healing Cloudflare Tunnel Engine**: Persistent zero-trust tunnel manager for instant live public access.
