CAMPAIGN STRATEGY & DELIVERY MATRIX

Campaign Execution Framework

From Strategic Planning to Successful Campaign Delivery

Every successful campaign starts with a clear business objective and ends with measurable results. My approach combines strategic planning, financial feasibility, partnership development, creative execution, integrated marketing, and continuous performance optimization to deliver a well-coordinated campaign from start to finish.

Phase 01

Strategy & Decision Making

01

Campaign Planning & Feasibility

Evaluate the campaign opportunity and determine whether to launch based on timing, duration, pricing, business objectives, customer demand, budget, and other key factors.

02

Partnership & Sponsor Mapping

Develop an initial, unofficial list of potential banks, sponsors, brands, and collaborators to explore partnership opportunities and assess interest.

03

Offer Strategy & Mechanics

Define the campaign proposition, including B1G1, B1G2, B1G3, selected bank-card offers, giveaways, discounts, and other promotional mechanics that create value for customers and the business.

Phase 02

Partnership & Financial Planning

04

Campaign Concept Development

Create an initial visual concept, theme, and design direction to communicate the campaign idea to potential sponsors and partners, helping establish expectations around sponsorship contributions, gifts, and collaboration.

05

Product Costing & ROI Planning

Evaluate product costs, promotional expenses, expected revenue, profit margins, sponsorship value, and target return on investment to ensure commercial viability.

06

Management Approval

Present the proposed campaign strategy, offer structure, budget, financial projections, and execution plan to senior management for review and approval before proceeding.

Phase 03

Creative Development & Approvals

07

Final Creative Production

Develop the complete campaign identity and execution assets, including event decor, social media creatives, online video commercials (OVC), promotional materials, signage, and other brand communication elements.

08

Bank & Gift Partner Approval

Coordinate with participating banks and gift partners to secure approval for campaign mechanics, terms and conditions, creative assets, branding, and promotional commitments.

Phase 04

Marketing & Event Readiness

09

Digital Marketing Activation

Launch the digital marketing campaign through social media, targeted advertising, content publishing, and coordinated promotional communication to build awareness and anticipation.

10

Event Setup & Decoration

Bring the campaign concept to life through physical event preparation, branded installations, decoration, signage, displays, and on-site promotional elements.

11

Media, Influencer & VIP Outreach

Coordinate invitations and engagement with media representatives, bloggers, influencers, celebrities, and VIP clients to strengthen campaign visibility and launch participation.

Phase 05

Launch & PR Activation

12

Campaign Launch & Public Relations

Execute the official campaign launch, activate the promotional offer, coordinate launch-day activities, and create public relations opportunities to maximize initial attention and engagement.

13

Collaborative Media & Blogger Marketing

Work collaboratively with media outlets, bloggers, and influencers to extend campaign reach, amplify the campaign message, and sustain audience interest throughout the campaign period.

Phase 06

Performance Optimization & Closure

14

Daily KPI Monitoring & Optimization

Track daily campaign performance through relevant KPIs and marketing analytics. Identify opportunities, address performance gaps, optimize ongoing activities, and translate insights into actionable improvements.

15

Campaign Closure & Results

Conclude the campaign with a closing celebration, announce giveaway or prize winners where applicable, review overall performance, document key learnings, and identify opportunities for future campaigns.

Core Philosophy

My Approach

Plan Strategically. Collaborate Effectively. Execute Creatively. Optimize Continuously.

I approach every campaign as an integrated business initiative—aligning commercial objectives, partner expectations, customer value, creative communication, and measurable performance to create a cohesive campaign experience.
