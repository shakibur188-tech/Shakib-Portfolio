fae

Online Rental App

UX CASE STUDY

Fae Rental is platform where you can renting furniture, Appliances & Electronics in an affordable prices

Problem Statement

We found out that a lot of people like Bachelors, Paying Guests, working professionals who relocate need furniture, electronics, and appliances. Because of the high cost and no certainty of staying in a place they avoid buying them. These people need the items for a shorter duration. This app will help them to rent the items for what period they want (a week, a month, a year). This includes renting the refurbished & recycled items.

The Solution

  • Introducing a renting platform for furniture, appliances & electronics.
  • The user can choose their desired tenure for renting (Week, Months, or one year).
  • Can easily relocate the rented items from our app for free.
  • Can swap the rented item for upgradation.
  • No delivery charges will be applicable for renting items.
  • Easy cleanup & maintenance.
