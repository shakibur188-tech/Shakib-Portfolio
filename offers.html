TRANSPARENT SPRINT TERMS

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can we kick off our campaign or platform sprint?

Once an offer is confirmed and brief intake is completed, sprint onboarding happens within 24 to 48 hours. We set up shared communication channels and commence architecture immediately.

What are the milestone payment terms?

All fixed-scope builds follow a transparent 50/50 milestone schedule (50% commencement deposit, 50% upon verified production deployment). Custom monthly retainers are invoiced at the beginning of each 30-day sprint.

Can a sprint package be tailored for our specific business model?

Yes. Every enterprise is unique. We frequently customize hybrid sprints that combine paid ads scaling with bespoke web architecture or brand repositioning.

Do we own 100% of our code, creative assets, and ad accounts?

Absolutely. You maintain 100% intellectual property ownership of your codebase, design source files, domain assets, and ad account billing. Zero vendor lock-in.
